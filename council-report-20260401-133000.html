Question: 目前的 Crypto Pulse 策略有沒有問題？
Strategy: VWAP + Bollinger Bands + RSI mean-reversion on 4h crypto candles

Chairman Synthesis — Decision Memo

Recommendation

暫停實盤部署。以下三個修正完成前不得重新上線。

Fix 1 — 非做不可 (最高優先)

Only evaluate signals on confirmed 4h candle closes. The engine ticks every 5 minutes on a live, unconfirmed candle. RSI, BB, VWAP computed on intracandle noise can fire a buy signal from a wick spike that fully recovers before candle close — invisible in backtest, real in live. Estimated 30–50% live performance degradation from this alone. Fix: only compute signals when a new candle has opened, using the prior candle's confirmed OHLC.

Fix 2 — 高優先

Add a volatility regime filter. 20-day realized vol vs. 60-day realized vol ratio. When short-term vol > long-term vol by >1.3× (trending/expanding regime): strategy parks flat. When roughly equal (consolidating): entries allowed. Addresses the unanimous convergence on trending-market failure without requiring a strategy rebuild.

Fix 3 — 中優先 (可議)

Replace OR entry with AND confirmation. Require RSI < 35 AND price < BB lower band simultaneously. Eliminates signal pollution from RSI-only entries during genuine trend collapses. Accept reduced trade frequency. Note: If Fix 2 is implemented, Fix 3 may be optional — trending-regime entries would already be blocked.

Points of Agreement (4+ advisors)

  • Trending-market regime blindness — Strategy has no off-switch for trending markets. Buys into momentum breakdowns calling them "value." Advisors A, B, C, D all converge here from different angles.
  • Backtest validity is thin — Two correlated assets (SOL/BNB) in one structural bull year (2024) is survivor selection, not validation. A and D name this independently.
  • Exit asymmetry runs backwards — RSI > 65 cuts winners prematurely in trending markets while losers run to the full ATR stop. B, D, E each identify this independently.
  • OR entry condition = signal pollution — RSI < 35 alone fires during genuine trend collapses with no BB confirmation required. B and D flag this independently.

Advisor Responses

The Contrarian — 找致命缺陷

The +13.5% SOL backtest is a mirage. 2024 was a textbook mean-reversion year for crypto — range-bound consolidations, predictable bounces, low-volatility drift upward. Run this on 2022 or the May 2021 crash and watch ATR-based stops detonate sequentially as every "oversold" signal becomes a trap door to the next leg down.

The fatal flaw: mean reversion fails catastrophically in trending markets, and crypto trends violently. RSI < 35 in a genuine downtrend is not oversold — it's a warning. The strategy has no trend filter. EMA200, SuperTrend, nothing. It buys into momentum breakdowns and calls it "value."

The ATR stop is a false sense of precision. ATR expands after a sharp move, meaning the stop widens exactly when you need it tight. You enter on a spike down, ATR balloons, your stop is now 4% below entry — you've engineered maximum loss on maximum volatility.

No take profit is reckless, not clever. In a slow bleed, neither RSI > 65 nor price > VWAP ever triggers. You hold a losing position indefinitely while the strategy keeps recalculating "not yet overbought."

Backtesting on two coins across one favorable year proves nothing. SOL and BNB in 2024 had specific macro tailwinds. This is survivor selection disguised as validation.

The First Principles Thinker — 從根本重建

What are we actually solving? Catching temporary dislocations from fair value and exiting when price normalizes. Clean premise. Now stress-test it.

Flaw 1: VWAP on 4h is meaningless as "fair value." A rolling 24-bar VWAP on 4h data is just a 4-day volume-weighted moving average. In a trending regime — which crypto spends most of its time in — price stays below VWAP for weeks. You're buying knives falling through an arbitrary reference line.

Flaw 2: OR condition creates signal pollution. RSI < 35 OR price < BB lower fires on two structurally different regimes. RSI oversold in a crash looks identical to RSI oversold in a healthy pullback. Combining with OR without regime distinction guarantees catastrophic entries during capitulation events.

Flaw 3: No take profit is wrong for mean reversion. Mean reversion strategies require a profit target — that's definitionally what mean reversion is. Without TP, you're holding a mean-reversion position hoping it becomes a trend trade. That's indecision baked into code.

Hidden risk: ATR SL during volatility spikes gets blown through on gaps. 4h candles gap at open. Your SL is a level, not a guaranteed fill.

The Expansionist — 找上行機會

The design is fundamentally sound. Mean reversion with multi-layer confirmation (RSI + BB + VWAP) on 4h is exactly the right frequency — enough signal density, low enough fee drag. The ATR-based stop is the smartest decision in the whole system. Stop second-guessing it.

Biggest untapped lever: dynamic position sizing. Flat $1000 per trade regardless of signal quality is amateur hour. When RSI drops below 25 AND price breaks BB lower AND price is 3%+ below VWAP simultaneously — triple-confluence screams for 2x–3x size. Build a signal strength score and watch Sharpe improve without touching core logic.

Adjacent opportunity: cross-asset mean reversion pairs. SOL and BNB both triggering simultaneously is a correlated drawdown. Weight the one further from its VWAP more heavily. You're already collecting both signals; use the relationship.

The hidden risk nobody's seeing: regime blindness. This strategy dies in trending markets. You need a volatility regime filter (20-day realized vol vs. 60-day) to park the strategy in cash when trends run hot. That's where the next +5–8% lives.

Stop optimizing parameters. Build the regime filter and dynamic sizing — that's where the real alpha is.

The Outsider — 局外人視角

The strategy's core premise — that crypto mean-reverts — is the first thing an outsider questions. SOL and BNB in 2024 were in a structural bull market. Mean reversion "working" in a bull market is just buying dips. The 13.5% SOL return proves 2024 was a good year to be long SOL, not that the strategy logic is sound.

Exit design is backwards. You removed fixed TP but replaced it with nothing except a weak overbought signal. RSI > 65 in a trending market fires constantly and prematurely. You're letting losers run to the ATR stop while cutting winners the moment RSI breathes above 65. The asymmetry runs backwards.

The ATR stop is naïve. Set at entry and never adjusts. A position that moves 8% in your favor can still give back everything if price reverses — no trailing mechanism exists.

OR logic is sloppy. RSI < 35 alone fires during genuine trend collapses with no BB confirmation. You're entering breakdowns just as readily as oversold bounces.

Two correlated assets in one favorable year is not validation. Run this on 2022. Until then, the backtest is decorative.

The Executor — 週一早上會發生什麼

Signal pollution from intracandle sampling — this is the most dangerous flaw. The engine runs every 5 minutes against a 4h candle that has NOT closed. RSI, BB, and VWAP are computed on a live, still-forming candle. A wick spike can trigger RSI < 35, fire a buy, then recover fully before the candle closes. In backtest, signals only fire on confirmed closes. In live trading, you are entering on noise the backtest never saw. Estimated 30–50% live performance degradation from this alone.

No take profit is a live-trading liability. In choppy markets the exit signal either never fires (hold a losing position through ATR SL) or fires prematurely on a brief RSI > 65 spike. The ATR SL at 1.5x gives roughly 2-3% downside on 4h SOL. One bad gap-down candle eats three average winners.

Single-position constraint accumulates friction. Only one open position at a time means repeated entry/exit cycles in prolonged mean-reversion setups. Transaction costs compound fast.

Bottom line: Fix the intracandle sampling first or live results will run 30-50% below backtest expectations. Everything else is secondary.
